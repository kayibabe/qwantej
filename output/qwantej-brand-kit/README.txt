MatchSlip BRAND KIT

Migrated 2026-11-25 from the legacy QwanEj brand kit. The binary PNG/ICO assets in this folder are legacy QwanEj-generated assets and were not regenerated during this migration; replace them when MatchSlip-native artwork is produced.

Brand direction (MatchSlip):
- Primary: emerald #047857
- Neutrals: slate (#0f172a text, #64748b muted, #e2e8f0 borders, #f8fafc background, white surfaces)
- Typography: DM Sans (body), Plus Jakarta Sans (headings), JetBrains Mono (numeric/data)
- Tagline: Built on evidence, not hunches.

Light and dark folders each contain:
- logo.png: full stacked logo with tagline, on a solid theme background.
- icon-master.png: high resolution rounded app icon.
- icon PNGs: 128, 180 (Apple touch), 192, 256 and 512 pixels.
- favicon PNGs: 16, 32, 48 and 64 pixels.
- favicon.ico: embedded 16, 32, 48 and 64 pixel versions.

Notes:
- Raster PNG/ICO assets; no vector master.
- Favicons are downscaled app marks and retain reference detail.
- Generated geometry may vary slightly across variants.
- Legacy filenames/folder name are retained to avoid breaking references; content describes MatchSlip branding.
